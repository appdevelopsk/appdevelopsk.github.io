Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-07. Last updated: 2026-05-07.

This Privacy Policy describes how SK APPS (operated by Kenichiro Sakamoto, the "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit the websites appdevelopsk.com and tools.appdevelopsk.com (the "Site"). This policy applies to the Site only. Each individual mobile application listed on the Site has its own privacy policy located at its dedicated support page or store listing.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

The Site does not require an account. You can browse anonymously. You may choose to contact us by email; in that case we receive whatever information you include in your message.

1.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our servers and third-party services automatically receive standard request information, including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Browser type, operating system, screen size, and referring URL
  • Pages viewed, time spent, and clicks
  • Device identifiers and language settings

This information is used in aggregate to understand traffic and improve the Site.

1.3 Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage to remember your language preference, store consent choices, and measure traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time; some non-essential features may not work as expected.

2. Third-party services

2.1 Analytics

We may use Google Analytics (operated by Google LLC) to measure traffic and usage patterns. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about how visitors use the Site. The information generated is transmitted to and stored by Google. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

2.2 Advertising

We may serve advertisements on the Site through Google AdSense and other approved advertising partners. These partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) and similar technologies to serve relevant ads, including interest-based advertising based on your prior visits to this Site or other sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

2.3 Affiliate networks

Some links on tool pages, marked with a “Sponsored” badge, are affiliate links. If you click and complete a qualifying action (sign-up, purchase) at the partner site, we may receive a commission. Your price is unchanged. Networks we use (or plan to use) include Amazon Associates, A8.net, Moshimo Affiliate, Rakuten Affiliate, ValueCommerce, and direct partner programs. These networks may set their own cookies for attribution.

2.4 Hosting and content delivery

The Site is hosted on virtual private servers and may be served through a content delivery network. These providers receive request metadata necessary to deliver the Site.

3. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • Understand traffic patterns and feature usage
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents
  • Serve advertisements relevant to your interests
  • Respond to inquiries you send us
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics, advertising) acting on our behalf under their own privacy terms
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
  • Business successors in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (notably the EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws), you have rights including:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Objection to processing or withdrawal of consent
  • Data portability
  • Lodging a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email app.develop.sk@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Consent for advertising (EU/UK/EEA)

If you visit from the EU, UK, or EEA, we use a Consent Management Platform compliant with Google's EU User Consent Policy and IAB TCF. You will see a consent banner where you can accept, reject, or customise advertising and analytics cookies.

7. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States and Japan. By using the Site, you consent to such transfers under appropriate safeguards.

9. Data retention

Server logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and analytics purposes. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics may be retained longer. Email correspondence is retained as long as needed to address your inquiry.

10. Security

We use HTTPS, modern TLS, and standard security practices. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but take reasonable steps to protect information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted on the Site.
