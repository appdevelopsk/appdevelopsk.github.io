Frequently asked questions

How do I start a trail?
Open Waypoint, tap the “Add habit” button, give it a name, an icon, a color, and how many times a day you want to do it. It appears on your Home trail right away.

How do I log a waypoint?
Tap the big button on a habit card whenever you complete it. A new waypoint lights up on that habit's trail, and your streak updates instantly.

I tapped by mistake — can I undo it?
Yes. Tap the undo icon next to a habit to remove the most recent waypoint logged today.

What is the Map screen?
Map shows your last 14 days as a winding trail — bigger waypoints mean more completions that day, and a small flag marks every 7-day milestone.

How do I set a daily reminder?
Waypoint can send a gentle reminder at 9:00 each day once you allow notifications in Settings.

Can I back up my data?
Yes — use “Export data” in Settings to share a JSON backup of your habits and log.
