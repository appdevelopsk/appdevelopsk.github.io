Gridkeep Support

The week grid

Gridkeep shows every habit as a row and every day of the week as a column. Tap a square to fill it, tap again to clear it. If a habit has a daily target above one, the square shows the count instead of a checkmark until the target is reached.

Adding and editing a habit

Tap the + button to add a habit — give it a name, an emoji and a color, and set how many times per day it should be done. Tap a habit's name on the grid to edit it later.

Looking at past weeks

Use the arrows above the grid to step back through previous weeks and see the pattern, not just a streak number. Future days are locked so you can't fill them in ahead of time.

History & Stats

Open History from the toolbar for a day-by-day list of everything logged. Open Stats for a longer-range view of how consistent each habit has been.

Reminders

Gridkeep sends a morning reminder so the grid doesn't go untouched. Reminders need notification permission — if you declined it, enable notifications for Gridkeep in the iOS Settings app.

Does my data leave the device?

No. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your habits and history live on this device only, so deleting the app deletes the data.

Restoring your purchase

The ad-free unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. On a new device, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
