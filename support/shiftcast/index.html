Setting up your rotation

Open My rotation, pick a preset close to your pattern or build the cycle a day at a time, then tap the circle next to the row that matches today. That single choice is what lines the projection up with your real schedule. A cycle can be up to 60 days long.

Why is the day after a night shift shown as time off?

Because it usually is. ShiftCast treats post-night rest as a rest day, so "your next two days off" matches how the break actually feels. If your workplace counts it differently, set that row to a work shift instead.

The projection is one day out

Almost always this means today is marked on the wrong row of the cycle. Reopen My rotation and move the "this is today" marker one row.

Reminders

The evening reminder names tomorrow's shift. It is scheduled on your device, so it keeps working with no signal — but iOS must have notification permission granted for ShiftCast in Settings. If you change your rotation, the upcoming reminders are rebuilt automatically.

Restoring your purchase

The ad-free unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. On a new device, open Premium and tap Restore purchase.
