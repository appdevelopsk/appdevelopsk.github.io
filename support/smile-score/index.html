FrameGrade

Photo analysis: see exactly why a shot looks off — and what to change

Download

FrameGrade is available for Android on Google Play: Get it on Google Play

How to Use

  • Pick a photo from your gallery
  • Five properties are measured from the pixels: exposure, lost detail, sharpness, colour cast and even lighting
  • Each finding shows the number behind it — how far underexposed, how soft, which corner is dark
  • Then one line of what to do differently next time

FAQ

  • A deliberately dark photo is marked as a problem FrameGrade measures the frame, not your intention. Read the finding and ignore it when the darkness is the point.
  • Is the result random or AI-guessed? No. There is no model and no randomness: the same photo always gives the same numbers, because each one is a direct property of its pixels.
  • Are my photos uploaded? No. Photos are read on your device, measured, and never written back, copied or sent anywhere.

Privacy

FrameGrade needs no account. Photos are processed locally on your device and never uploaded. We use anonymized analytics to improve the app experience.
