Billendar – Support

Billendar is a bill calendar and subscription-spending forecast app. See every recurring bill on a monthly calendar and get a 6-month spending forecast, so nothing renews as a surprise.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a bill?

Tap the + button on the Calendar or All Bills tab and fill in the service name, price, billing cycle, and next billing date.

Where is my data stored?

All bill data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings → Premium to remove ads via a one-time or annual purchase.
