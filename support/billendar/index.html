Billendar – Support

Billendar is a bill calendar and subscription-spending forecast app. See every recurring bill on a monthly calendar and get a 6-month spending forecast, so nothing renews as a surprise.

What you can do with Billendar

  • Track an unlimited number of subscriptions and recurring bills in one place
  • See every billing date on a monthly calendar
  • Get a forecast of your spending for the next 6 months
  • Check your monthly and annual cost overview
  • Receive renewal reminders as local notifications, so a renewal never surprises you
  • Spot subscriptions you have forgotten about and decide what to cancel

Getting started

  1. Tap the + button on the Calendar or All Bills tab.
  2. Enter the service name, price, billing cycle, and next billing date, then save.
  3. Open the calendar to see when each bill is due, and check the forecast to see what you will spend over the coming months.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a bill?

Tap the + button on the Calendar or All Bills tab and fill in the service name, price, billing cycle, and next billing date.

Is there a limit on how many bills I can add?

No. You can track unlimited subscriptions and bills.

How do renewal reminders work?

Billendar sends local notifications from your device to remind you of upcoming billing dates. You can change your notification preferences, default currency, and language in the app's settings.

Where is my data stored?

All bill data stays on your device in encrypted local storage and is never sent to our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete a bill or all of my data?

You can delete individual bills within the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. Because bill data never leaves your device, we cannot restore it after the app is uninstalled.

Does Billendar collect any other data?

The app uses Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage analytics and Sentry for crash reporting (device type and OS version). Ads are served by Google AdMob. We do not sell your personal information.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings → Premium to remove ads via a one-time or annual purchase.

Is Billendar for children?

No. The app is not directed to children under 13.
