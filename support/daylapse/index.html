Daylapse – Support & Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Daylapse turns one photo a day into a timelapse of your own life. Every photo stays on the device that took it.

Support

  • Email: app.develop.sk@gmail.com
  • Response time: within 2 business days

For a bug report, please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

Where are my photos stored? In the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, and there is no account to sign in to.

What happens if I miss a day? Nothing is lost. The calendar simply leaves that day empty and the timelapse skips it.

How do I export a timelapse? Once three or more days are captured, open Timelapse from the top bar and choose export. The result is saved as a GIF to your photo library.

What does Daylapse Premium include? Unlimited photo storage and export of your full history. The free version keeps the most recent 7 days. It is an auto-renewing subscription; you can cancel any time in the App Store subscription settings.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes every photo it stored. Individual days can be deleted from the calendar.

Privacy Policy

What Daylapse collects

Daylapse does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. Photos you take stay in the app's storage on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take the daily photo. Used only while the capture screen is open.
  • Photo library (add only) — to save a photo or an exported timelapse when you ask for it. Daylapse does not read your existing library.
  • Notifications — optional. Used only for the daily reminder you set, and requested after your first photo, not at launch.

Analytics and diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Sentry to count anonymous usage events (such as which screens are opened) and to report crashes. These reports contain no photos and no personal identifiers. See Firebase and Sentry for their handling of that data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Daylapse never receives your payment details.

Children

Daylapse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
