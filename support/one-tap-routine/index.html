Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

Open the app. Sample routines may be shown for your first session. You can also tap Add routine, give it a name and daily target, then save. Tap a routine card when you complete that activity.

How do I see progress and streaks?

Use Stats to review completed routines and recent activity. Use History to look back at recorded completions. A weekly review is being tested separately and may not yet be available in released versions.

How do reminders work?

Reminders are optional and require notification permission. You can turn the daily reminder on or off in Settings. The default scheduled reminder is at 9:00 AM.

Does the app lock my phone or block other apps?

No. One Tap Routine tracks habits and daily targets. It is not a phone-locking, device-management or screen-blocking app.

Where is my routine data?

Your routine records are saved locally on your device. Settings lets you export a JSON backup, hide routine names with Privacy mode, or delete local data. Exported backups may contain private routine names, so share them only with someone you trust.

Can I restore an earlier purchase?

If you bought a premium upgrade previously, use Restore purchase in Settings. This does not start a new subscription. Availability depends on the store and your purchase history.

Why is there no store install button here?

We show store download links only after verifying the exact public listing. An unverified link is not a real way to install an app.
