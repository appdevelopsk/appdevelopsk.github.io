Quick mobile games for a short break

Not every break needs a long session. Decide whether you want to spot clues, give an opinion, solve a small logic problem, or answer trivia.

How to choose

  1. Want a visual surprise? Choose an image-guessing game where the picture becomes clearer as you play.
  2. Want to react and compare opinions? Choose a community voting game instead of a puzzle.
  3. Want a simple A-or-B choice? Pick a poll, vote and compare answers with friends in Dotchi.
  4. Want to think rather than react? Pick a logic challenge or a trivia quiz.

Apps to explore
