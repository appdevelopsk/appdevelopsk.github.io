Decision helper apps for choosing food, movies and activities

When choosing takes longer than doing, start with the kind of choice you need to make. These four SK Apps tools focus on different situations, from picking a restaurant to deciding what to do next.

Choose for your situation

  1. Start with your context. A restaurant picker is useful when your decision depends on nearby places. A movie picker is better when you already plan to watch something.
  2. Agree on boundaries first. Set a time limit, budget or preferred category before you spin. Picking faster is only helpful if the option fits your situation.
  3. Avoid comparing unrelated tools. A choice wheel will not verify local restaurant hours; a food finder will not tell you which movie fits your mood. Open the store description for current capabilities.
