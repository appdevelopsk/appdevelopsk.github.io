Instant Judge Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Instant Judge ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Instant Judge mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Device identifier: A randomly generated anonymous device ID (UUID) used to identify your posts and votes. This ID cannot be linked to your real identity.
  • User-generated content: Posts (title and body text) and comments you submit through the app.
  • Voting data: Your votes on posts within the app.
  • Usage data: App activity such as posts viewed, votes cast, and comments made.
  • Crash and error data: Technical information collected automatically when the app encounters errors.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core Instant Judge service (posting, voting, commenting)
  • To display community judgment results and statistics
  • To show relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or inappropriate content
  • To monitor app performance and fix bugs (via Sentry)
  • To improve the app experience over time

3. Anonymity

Instant Judge is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require account registration, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information. Your identity is represented only by an anonymous device ID generated on your device. You are completely anonymous to other users.

4. Third-Party Services

Instant Judge uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob – for serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry – for crash reporting and error monitoring. Crash reports may include device information and stack traces. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

Posts, votes, and comments you create are stored on our servers until you delete them or until they are removed for violating our content policy. Crash reports are retained for 90 days.

6. Content Moderation

Users can report posts or comments that violate our community guidelines. Posts with a high number of reports may be automatically hidden pending review. We reserve the right to remove content that violates our policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Instant Judge is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and secure server storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access your data
  • The right to delete your data (posts, comments, and voting history)
  • The right to opt out of targeted advertising (via device settings)

To exercise these rights, contact us at app.develop.sk@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

app.develop.sk@gmail.com
