LifeBoard - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

LifeBoard collects the following information:

  • Birth date and settings: Stored locally on your device to calculate life statistics. This data is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Bucket list, milestones, and reviews: Stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Device information: Anonymous device identifiers for crash reporting and analytics.
  • Usage data: App interaction data for improving user experience.
  • Advertising identifiers: Used by Google AdMob to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your consent.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality
  • To display daily quotes and life expectancy data
  • To serve advertisements (free version)
  • To monitor and fix crashes via Sentry
  • To improve the app experience

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase: For app analytics and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy
  • Sentry: For error tracking and crash reporting. Sentry Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

All personal data (birth date, bucket list items, milestones, weekly reviews) is stored locally on your device. We do not store personal data on our servers. Our backend server only provides daily quotes and life expectancy reference data.

5. Data Retention

Your data is stored on your device until you delete the app or clear app data. Anonymous crash reports are retained for 90 days.

6. Children's Privacy

LifeBoard is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. In-App Purchases

LifeBoard offers a one-time purchase to remove advertisements. Purchase transactions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store payment information.

8. Your Rights

Since all personal data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all data by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page.
