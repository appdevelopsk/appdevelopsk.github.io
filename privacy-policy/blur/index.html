Blur – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Blur ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application Blur ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Device Identifier: A randomly generated device ID to create your anonymous account.
  • Username: An optional display name you choose.
  • Game Data: Your scores, tap counts, correct/incorrect results, and play history.
  • Device Information: Device type and OS version, used for analytics and error reporting via Sentry.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App (Daily Challenge, Infinite Mode, leaderboard).
  • To display your rank on the global leaderboard.
  • To improve the App through anonymous analytics.
  • To detect and fix errors using Sentry crash reporting.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We display interstitial and rewarded video ads.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google AdMob – for advertising purposes.
  • Sentry – for crash reporting and error monitoring.
  • AWS – for image storage (game images only, no user data).

5. Data Retention

Game results and leaderboard data are retained as long as you use the App. You can request deletion by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

This app displays ads served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation platform operated by Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity LevelPlay SDK and the ad networks it works with may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, operating system version, language setting, and your interactions with ads. This information is processed by Unity and its ad network partners under their own privacy policies: Unity Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: app.develop.sk@gmail.com
