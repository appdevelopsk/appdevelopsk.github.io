Privacy Policy

Meetlog Debrief · Effective date: July 30, 2026

Meetlog Debrief ("the app", "we") is a meeting reflection journal. We respect your privacy. Your content and settings are stored on your device, and the items listed below are also sent to our server so the app can work.

Data we process

  • On-device data: your settings and local history stay on your device.
  • Sent to our server: the meeting notes and reflections you choose to save, so they can be synced and summarised by our server.
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): aggregated, non-identifying usage and event data, to understand which features are used.
  • Crash & error reporting (Sentry): diagnostic information when the app crashes or hits an error, used solely to fix bugs.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): AdMob may use a device advertising identifier to serve ads. On iOS this only happens with your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, advertising tracking is not used.
  • In-app purchases: processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment details.

How data is used

Data is used only to operate and improve Meetlog Debrief — to deliver the features described above, to measure usage in aggregate and diagnose crashes, and to show advertising. We do not sell your personal data.

Children

Meetlog Debrief is designed for general audiences (13+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party providers

For details on how these providers handle data, see the Google (Firebase & AdMob) and Sentry privacy policies. Apple and Google handle in-app purchases under their own terms.

Your choices

  • Decline ad tracking at the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, or reset your advertising identifier in system settings.
  • Purchase the premium upgrade to remove advertising.
  • Ask us to delete the data held for you by writing to the contact address below.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data held on the device.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
