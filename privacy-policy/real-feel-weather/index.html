Real Feel Weather – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Real Feel Weather ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application Real Feel Weather ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Data: Approximate location (with your permission) to fetch weather data for your current area. Location data is used only for weather queries and is not stored on our servers.
  • Device Information: Device type and OS version, used for analytics and error reporting via Sentry.
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the App.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements (with your permission via App Tracking Transparency).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App, including displaying weather information and "real feel" conditions.
  • To send local notification reminders about weather conditions (configurable in settings).
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues via Sentry crash reporting.
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve features via Firebase Analytics.

3. Weather Data

The App retrieves weather data from third-party weather services using your approximate location. No weather queries or location data are stored on our servers beyond the current session.

4. Notifications

The App may send local notifications as weather reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any server communication. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following third-party service providers:

  • Google AdMob: For advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google): For analytics. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Sentry: For crash reporting. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

The App does not store personal data on our servers. Analytics and crash data are retained according to our service providers' policies.

7. Your Rights

You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's privacy settings or by responding to the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You may contact us to request information about data we hold about you.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.
