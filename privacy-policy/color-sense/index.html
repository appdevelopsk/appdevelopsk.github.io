Color Sense – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Color Sense ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application Color Sense ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Game Data: Your scores, accuracy, streaks, and play history are stored locally on your device only.
  • Device Information: Device type and OS version, used for analytics and error reporting via Firebase Analytics and Sentry.
  • Advertising Identifier: If you grant permission, your device's advertising identifier may be collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) to serve personalized ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's functionality
  • To track your progress and display statistics (locally on your device)
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To monitor app stability and fix crashes via Sentry
  • To understand usage patterns via Firebase Analytics

3. Data Storage

All game data (scores, streaks, levels, XP) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. We do not have a backend server and do not collect or store your game data on any remote server.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Displays ads and may collect device identifiers and usage data. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous usage data. Firebase Privacy
  • Sentry: Collects crash reports and error data. Sentry Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not require account creation or personal data input.

6. Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS.
  • You can delete all local data by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App or on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at appdevelopsk@gmail.com.
