Privacy Policy

Tracepedia — Last updated: July 29, 2026

Overview

Tracepedia is a reference app containing three encyclopedias — wild animals, birds and the constellations. You browse entries, compare them, take quizzes and keep notes. Everything you create stays on your device. There is no account to make and nothing is uploaded.

Your Data

Your notes, quiz results, collection progress and settings are stored only on your device. Tracepedia has no account system and no server of its own. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics for anonymized usage data (such as which screens are opened) and Sentry for crash reports, so we can fix problems. Neither receives your notes, and neither identifies you.

Advertising

This version of Tracepedia does not show ads and does not initialize any advertising SDK. If ads are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and on iOS you will be asked for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before any tracking takes place.

Purchases

Tracepedia offers an optional in-app purchase that unlocks the full content. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

Tracepedia can send you an optional daily reminder. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings, and it is off until you allow it.

Children's Privacy

Tracepedia is suitable for all ages and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
