Movie Roulette – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Movie Roulette ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application Movie Roulette ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Usage Data: App usage patterns and spin history are stored anonymously on your device. No personally identifiable information is stored on our servers.
  • Device Information: Device type and OS version, used for analytics and error reporting via Sentry.
  • Crash Reports: Diagnostic data to help us identify and fix errors (via Sentry).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App.
  • To improve the App through anonymous analytics (Firebase Analytics).
  • To detect and fix errors using Sentry crash reporting.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency permission before showing personalized ads.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (Premium subscription). Purchase transactions are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store payment information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google AdMob – for advertising purposes.
  • Sentry – for crash reporting and error monitoring.
  • Firebase (Google) – for anonymous usage analytics.

6. Data Retention

App preferences and filter settings are stored locally on your device. You can clear this data by uninstalling the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

9. Contact Us
