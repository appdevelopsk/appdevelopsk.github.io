Snap Judge Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snap Judge ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Snap Judge mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device identifier: A randomly generated anonymous device ID (UUID) used to track your game progress and scores. This ID cannot be linked to your real identity.
  • Game data: Your answers, scores, and session history within the app.
  • Leaderboard data: Optional username and score for display on global leaderboards.
  • Crash and error data: Technical information collected automatically when the app encounters errors (via Sentry).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core Snap Judge game experience
  • To track your scores and game history
  • To display global leaderboards
  • To show relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To monitor app performance and fix bugs
  • To improve the app experience over time

3. Anonymity

Snap Judge does not require account registration or any personally identifiable information. Your identity is represented only by an anonymous device ID generated on your device. Leaderboard usernames are optional and chosen by you.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob – for serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry – for crash reporting and error monitoring. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

Game scores and history are stored on our servers. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us. Crash reports are retained for 90 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Snap Judge is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and secure server storage.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us at app.develop.sk@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.

10. Contact

app.develop.sk@gmail.com
