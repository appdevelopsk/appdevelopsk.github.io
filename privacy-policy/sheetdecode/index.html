Privacy Policy

SheetDecode — Last updated: July 25, 2026

Overview

SheetDecode explains spreadsheet formulas. Every formula you paste or type is parsed on your device and is never transmitted, stored on a server, or logged by us. There is no account and no sign-in.

Information You Enter

Formulas you decode stay in memory on your device for the length of your session. We do not save them, sync them, or send them anywhere.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics for anonymized usage data (such as which screens are opened) and Sentry / Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, so we can fix problems. Neither receives the content of the formulas you decode, and neither identifies you.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with Google's privacy policy. On iOS we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before any tracking. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads.

Children's Privacy

SheetDecode is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page.
