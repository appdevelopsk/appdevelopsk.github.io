Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Key Point: Win Log Achievement Tracker is completely anonymous. We do not collect any personal information. No account, no email, no name required.

1. Information We Collect

Win Log Achievement Tracker collects the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Dream text — the content you write and choose to share
  • Device ID — a randomly generated UUID stored on your device. This is used solely to attribute your dreams to you (so you can view your own history). It is never linked to your identity.
  • Country code — derived from your device locale (e.g., "JP", "US"). Used only for the world map feature. Never an exact location.
  • AI analysis results — mood and tags generated by AI from your dream text

We do not collect: name, email, phone number, precise location, IP address (beyond standard server logs), or any identifying information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Display your dream history within the app (device ID only)
  • Show aggregated, anonymous dream trends and world map data
  • Improve AI analysis quality

We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. AI Analysis

When you submit a dream, the text is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI API for analysis. The AI identifies mood and thematic tags. Anthropic's data processing is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

Your dream text and AI-generated tags are shared publicly in aggregated, anonymous form (world map and trends). Individual dreams may appear in global feeds without any personally identifiable information.

If you do not wish your dream to be shared, simply do not submit it.

5. Data Retention

Dreams you submit are stored on our servers indefinitely for trend analysis. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us with your device ID (found in the app settings).

6. Children's Privacy

Win Log Achievement Tracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission. Server infrastructure is secured with industry-standard practices. No payment information is collected or stored.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
