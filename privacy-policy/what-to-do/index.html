Privacy Policy — What To Do: Activity Picker

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

What To Do: Activity Picker collects anonymous usage analytics (screen views, feature interactions) via Firebase Analytics to improve the app experience. No personally identifiable information is collected or stored.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization, subject to your device's tracking preferences and App Tracking Transparency consent.

Data Storage

User preferences and history are stored locally on your device only and are not transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC)
  • Sentry (error monitoring)

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

This app displays ads served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation platform operated by Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity LevelPlay SDK and the ad networks it works with may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, operating system version, language setting, and your interactions with ads. This information is processed by Unity and its ad network partners under their own privacy policies: Unity Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
