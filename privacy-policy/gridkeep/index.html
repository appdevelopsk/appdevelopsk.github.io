Privacy Policy

Gridkeep — Last updated: August 7, 2026

Overview

Gridkeep is a weekly habit tracker: it shows your habits as a grid, one row per habit and one column per day, so you can tap a square to log progress. Your habits, daily entries and history are all stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.

Your Data

Habit names, emojis, colors, daily targets, completion entries and reminder settings are stored only on your device. Gridkeep has no account system and uploads nothing. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics for anonymized usage data (such as which screens are opened) and Sentry for crash reports, so we can fix problems. Neither receives your habit names, entries, or history, and neither identifies you.

Advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with Google's privacy policy. On iOS we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before any tracking. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads.

Children's Privacy

Gridkeep is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page.
