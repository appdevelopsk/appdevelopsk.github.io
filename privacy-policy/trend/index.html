Privacy Policy

Trend — Last updated: September 25, 2026

Information We Collect

Trend collects your mood preferences, experience level, and viewing history to provide personalized recommendations for movies, TV shows, and games. This data is stored on our servers to improve your recommendations over time.

How We Use Your Information

Your preferences and interaction data are used solely to generate personalized recommendations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized usage data (screen views, feature usage) to improve the app. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Data Retention

Your preference data is stored on our servers as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect or store any payment information.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

This app displays ads served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation platform operated by Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity LevelPlay SDK and the ad networks it works with may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, operating system version, language setting, and your interactions with ads. This information is processed by Unity and its ad network partners under their own privacy policies: Unity Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
