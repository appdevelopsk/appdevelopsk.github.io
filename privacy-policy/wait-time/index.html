Wait Time – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wait Time ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application Wait Time ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password to authenticate you. You may also browse wait time data without an account.
  • Wait Time Reports: Wait time and crowding data you submit for restaurants, attractions, and other venues.
  • Device Information: Device type and OS version, used for analytics and error reporting via Sentry.
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the App.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements (with your permission via App Tracking Transparency).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App, including displaying live wait time information.
  • To authenticate your account.
  • To send local notification reminders (configurable in settings).
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues via Sentry crash reporting.
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve features via Firebase Analytics.

3. Notifications

The App may send local notifications as daily reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any server communication. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

4. Account Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App (tap the menu icon > Delete Account). Upon deletion, your account and submitted reports will be permanently removed from our servers.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following third-party service providers:

  • Google AdMob: For advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google): For analytics. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Sentry: For crash reporting. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account. Analytics and crash data are retained according to our service providers' policies.

7. Your Rights

You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's privacy settings or by responding to the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You may request deletion of your account data through the App or by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at: app.develop.sk@gmail.com
