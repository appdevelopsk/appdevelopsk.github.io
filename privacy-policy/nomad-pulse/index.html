Nomad Pulse – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nomad Pulse ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application Nomad Pulse ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your display name and email address to create and manage your account. You may also browse spots as a guest without providing any personal information.
  • User-Generated Content: Work spot reports, ratings, and comments you submit to help other digital nomads find great work spots.
  • Location Data: Approximate location (with your permission) to show nearby work spots. We do not store precise location data on our servers.
  • Device Information: Device type and OS version, used for analytics and error reporting via Sentry.
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App, including displaying and managing work spot listings.
  • To authenticate your account and manage your saved spots and reports.
  • To send local notification reminders (configurable in settings).
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues via Sentry crash reporting.
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve features via Firebase Analytics.

3. User-Generated Content

The App allows users to submit work spot reports and ratings. By submitting content, you confirm that you are at least 13 years of age and that your content complies with our Community Guidelines. We reserve the right to remove content that violates our guidelines.

4. Notifications

The App may send local notifications as reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any server communication. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following third-party service providers:

  • Firebase (Google): For analytics. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Sentry: For crash reporting. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen within the App. Upon account deletion, your personal information and submitted content will be removed from our servers within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account. Analytics and crash data are retained according to our service providers' policies.

8. Your Rights

You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's privacy settings or by responding to the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You may request deletion of your account data through the App or by contacting us at the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users must confirm they are at least 13 years old during registration.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to delete your account, please contact us at: app.develop.sk@gmail.com
