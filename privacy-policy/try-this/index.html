Privacy Policy

TryThis – AI Life Recorder  |  Effective date: March 2026

Your privacy matters to us. TryThis collects only the data it needs to generate your personalized daily life summaries and happiness score. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

TryThis collects the following categories of data when you use the app:

Category Examples Purpose
Health Data Step count, walking/running distance, sleep duration and quality, active energy, exercise minutes Generate your AI daily life summary and happiness score
Location Data Places visited during the day (city/venue level); location history used to build your Life Map Create your daily narrative and Life Map visualization
Usage Data App screens viewed, features used, crash reports, device model and OS version Improve app stability and user experience
Account Data Email address (if you create an account), device identifier Associate your life data with your account and sync across devices

Health data is read from Apple Health (HealthKit) only after you grant explicit permission. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Location data is collected only when you grant location access in iOS Settings. We request "Always" access so that your daily journey can be captured automatically; however, the app works in reduced capacity with "While Using" access.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • AI daily summary generation: Your health and location data are sent to OpenAI's API to generate your personalized daily narrative. Data is transmitted in anonymized form — we do not send your name or email to OpenAI.
  • Happiness score calculation: Activity metrics are analyzed on our servers to compute your daily happiness score.
  • Life Map: Location history is stored and used to render your interactive map within the app.
  • Weekly insights: Aggregated data from your week is analyzed by AI to surface patterns and trends.
  • App analytics: Usage data is processed by Firebase Analytics to understand feature adoption and improve the app.
  • Error monitoring: Crash and error data is sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs quickly.
  • Push notifications: If you enable notifications, we use your device token to send daily reminder notifications. We do not send marketing messages.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers protected by industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest). We apply role-based access controls and routine security reviews to protect your information.

Certain data (generated summaries, happiness scores, Life Map history) is cached on your device for offline access. Device-local data is protected by iOS's built-in data protection mechanisms.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently delete all associated data within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

TryThis uses the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Service Provider Data Shared Purpose
Firebase Analytics Google LLC Usage events, device type, OS version (no personally identifiable information) App analytics and feature improvement — Privacy Policy
Sentry Functional Software, Inc. Crash reports, error logs, device model (no health or location data) Error tracking and crash monitoring — Privacy Policy
OpenAI API OpenAI, L.L.C. Anonymized health metrics and location context (no name or email; not used to train AI models) AI-generated daily life summaries — Privacy Policy

We do not share your data with advertising networks, data brokers, or any other third parties beyond those listed above.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access: You can view your stored data within the app at any time.
  • Correction: You can edit manually added information through the app settings.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of all your data by contacting us at the email below. We will process your request within 30 days.
  • Health data revocation: Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → TryThis and disable access.
  • Location revocation: Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → TryThis.
  • Notifications: Disable push notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → TryThis at any time.
  • Analytics opt-out: Firebase Analytics respects the iOS "Limit Ad Tracking" system setting.

6. Children's Privacy

TryThis is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. International Data Transfers

Our servers are located in Japan. If you use TryThis from outside Japan, your data may be transferred to and processed in Japan. By using the app, you consent to this transfer. All transfers are protected by the security measures described in Section 3.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. The "Effective date" at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of TryThis after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
