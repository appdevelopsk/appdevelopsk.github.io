TAKE SIDES Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TAKE SIDES ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the TAKE SIDES mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from those providers.
  • Usage data: Your votes, battle results, dilemmas created, and app activity.
  • Device information: Device ID (for anonymous user identification), operating system, and app version.
  • Location data: Country-level location derived from your IP address for regional vote statistics. We do not collect precise GPS location.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the TAKE SIDES service
  • To display global and regional vote statistics
  • To generate your personality report based on your voting patterns
  • To operate the 1v1 battle matchmaking system
  • To show relevant advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Third-Party Services

TAKE SIDES uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob – for serving advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In – for optional account authentication.
  • Sign in with Apple – for optional account authentication on iOS.
  • Sentry – for crash reporting and error monitoring. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.

5. Children's Privacy

TAKE SIDES is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and secure storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to delete your data
  • The right to opt out of targeted advertising

To exercise these rights, contact us at app.develop.sk@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the TAKE SIDES app: go to Settings → Delete Account.

If you no longer have access to the app, please email us at app.develop.sk@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request - TAKE SIDES". We will delete your data within 30 days.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

app.develop.sk@gmail.com
