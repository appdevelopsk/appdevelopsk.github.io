Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Food Roulette: What to Eat ("Food Roulette", "the App"; Android package name com.appdevelopsk.food_roulette), which is developed and published on Google Play by the developer appdevelopsk ("we", "us", "our"). It explains what data the App collects, how it is used and with whom it is shared.

Food Roulette respects your privacy. This app collects minimal data necessary for core functionality.

Location Data

Food Roulette asks for permission to access your device location (precise or approximate) so that it can find restaurants near you. Location is accessed only while you are using the App and only when you start a search; the App does not collect location in the background.

When you search, the coordinates of the search point and your selected filters (search radius and food genres) are sent over an encrypted connection to our server (api.appdevelopsk.com), operated by appdevelopsk, and, if our server is unavailable, directly to public OpenStreetMap Overpass API servers, in order to retrieve a list of nearby restaurants. Search results may be cached on your device and on our server for a limited time (up to 90 days on the server) to speed up later searches. Cached search data is not linked to your name, account, advertising identifier or any other identifier, and we do not use it to build a location history or profile of you.

We do not sell your location data and we do not use it for advertising. You can refuse or withdraw the location permission at any time in your device Settings; you can then still use the App by choosing a search location manually.

Data Collection

We use Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob to improve the app experience. These services may collect usage data and device identifiers in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

App Tracking Transparency

This app requests permission to track your activity for personalized advertising. You can opt out at any time in your device Settings.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

This app displays ads served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation platform operated by Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity LevelPlay SDK and the ad networks it works with may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, operating system version, language setting, and your interactions with ads. This information is processed by Unity and its ad network partners under their own privacy policies: Unity Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
