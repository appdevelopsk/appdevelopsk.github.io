Privacy Policy

PixelSpot - Image Compare — Developer: AppDevelop SK — Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AppDevelop SK ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile app PixelSpot - Image Compare ("PixelSpot", "the App") on Android and iOS.

Overview

PixelSpot compares two versions of a picture and shows what changed. The comparison runs entirely on your device. Your images are never uploaded, and there is no account to create. PixelSpot has no leaderboard, user profile or server-side storage of your data.

Your Images

When you choose two images, PixelSpot reads them to run the comparison and holds them in memory while the result is on screen. They are not copied into the app's own storage, not sent anywhere, and are released when you start over or close the app. We never see them.

Photo Access

On Android, PixelSpot uses the system photo picker, so the App only receives the images you select and cannot browse the rest of your photos. On iOS, the system asks your permission before PixelSpot can open your photo library. In both cases this access is used for one thing: letting you pick the two images you want to compare.

Settings Stored on Your Device

Your preferences (for example language, sound and reminder settings) are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us and are removed when you uninstall the App.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, PixelSpot schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to a server.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay, Android)

The Android version displays ads served through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation platform operated by Unity Technologies. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity LevelPlay SDK and the ad networks it works with may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, operating system version and language setting, and ad interaction data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See the Unity privacy policy.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config and In-App Messaging) for anonymized usage data (such as which screens are opened), app performance, configuration and crash reports, and Sentry for error reports, so we can fix problems. These services may process device information such as device model, OS version and an app instance identifier. None of them receives your images.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Sentry, Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partners) for the purposes described.

Data Retention and Deletion

Images are never stored. Settings stay on your device until you uninstall the App. Analytics and crash data are kept by the providers above according to their retention policies. To request deletion of any data associated with your device, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

PixelSpot is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page.
