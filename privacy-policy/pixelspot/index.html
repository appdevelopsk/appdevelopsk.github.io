Privacy Policy

PixelSpot — Last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

PixelSpot compares two versions of a picture and shows what changed. The comparison runs entirely on your device. Your images are never uploaded, and there is no account to create.

Your Images

When you choose two images, PixelSpot reads them to run the comparison and holds them in memory while the result is on screen. They are not copied into the app's own storage, not sent anywhere, and are released when you start over or close the app. We never see them.

Photo Library Access

iOS asks your permission before PixelSpot can open your photo library. That access is used for one thing: letting you pick the two images you want to compare. PixelSpot does not browse, index or upload your library.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics for anonymized usage data (such as which screens are opened) and Sentry / Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, so we can fix problems. Neither receives your images, and neither identifies you.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with Google's privacy policy. On iOS we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before any tracking. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads.

Children's Privacy

PixelSpot is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page.
