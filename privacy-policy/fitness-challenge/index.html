Privacy Policy — Daily Fitness Challenge

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

Daily Fitness Challenge collects anonymous usage analytics (screen views, feature interactions) via Firebase Analytics to improve the app experience. No personally identifiable information is collected or stored.

Data Storage

Your workout records, challenge progress, and app settings are stored locally on your device only and are not transmitted to any server.

Health Data

This app does not read from or write to Apple Health or Google Fit, and does not access any health or fitness data managed by your device.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent to or through our servers.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization, subject to your device's tracking preferences and App Tracking Transparency consent.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC)
  • Sentry (error monitoring)

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
