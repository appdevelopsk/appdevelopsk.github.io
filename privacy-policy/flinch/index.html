Flinch – Support & Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Flinch gives you one reflex game a day, drawn from twelve game types, and shows where your score lands among everyone who played the same game that day.

Support

  • Email: app.develop.sk@gmail.com
  • Response time: within 2 business days

For a bug report, please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

Why can I only play once a day? Because the ranking compares everyone on a single attempt. Unlimited retries would make the score meaningless, so the challenge resets at midnight in your own time zone.

I lost my streak — can it be restored? No. The streak counts consecutive days on which a challenge was completed, and it is kept on the device.

Which game will I get? The day's game is the same for everyone and is chosen by the server, so the ranking compares like with like.

Do I need an account? No. The app registers an anonymous device identifier on first launch and that is the only identity it uses.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. To have the server-side scores tied to your device removed, email the address above from the device in question.

Privacy Policy

What Flinch collects

Flinch does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and there is no sign-in. To place you in the ranking, the app sends the score of each completed challenge together with an anonymous identifier generated on the device. That identifier is not linked to you as a person, and the ranking displays it as an anonymous entry.

Permissions

  • Notifications — optional. Used only for the daily reminder and the streak warning you set, and requested after your first completed challenge, not at launch.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — you are asked whether to allow tracking. Declining changes nothing about how the game works.

Analytics and diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Sentry to count anonymous usage events (such as which screens are opened) and to report crashes. These reports contain no personal identifiers. See Firebase and Sentry for their handling of that data.

Advertising

Flinch contains no advertising.

Children

Flinch is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
