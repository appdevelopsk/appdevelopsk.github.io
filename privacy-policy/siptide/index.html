Privacy Policy

SipTide — Last updated: July 27, 2026

Overview

SipTide reminds you to drink water at an interval you choose, lets you log glasses with one tap, and shows your daily progress, streak and weekly stats. Everything is stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.

Your Data

Your glasses, goals, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. SipTide has no account system and uploads nothing. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics for anonymized usage data (such as which screens are opened) and Sentry / Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, so we can fix problems. Neither receives what you log, and neither identifies you.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with Google's privacy policy. On iOS we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before any tracking. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads.

Children's Privacy

SipTide is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page.
